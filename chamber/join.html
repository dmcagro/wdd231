NP Membership Benefits

  • Networking opportunities with other non-profit organizations.
  • Access to selected chamber events.
  • Opportunities to participate in community development activities.
  • Business community updates and information.

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Basic business networking opportunities.
  • Access to selected chamber events.
  • Business community updates.
  • Opportunity to connect with other members.

Silver Membership Benefits

  • All Bronze membership benefits.
  • Additional business promotion opportunities.
  • Priority access to selected workshops and seminars.
  • Enhanced networking opportunities.

Gold Membership Benefits

  • All Silver membership benefits.
  • Premium business promotion opportunities.
  • Priority invitations to selected business events.
  • Enhanced visibility within the chamber community.
  • Additional networking and partnership opportunities.
